How Image Adjust with Perspective Crop Works

  • Fix Camera Distortion & Tilted Angles: When you snap a photo of an Aadhaar card, PAN card, marksheet, or receipt with a smartphone, it is usually slanted or keystoned. This tool uses 4-point homography projection to mathematically flatten and unskew it into a rectangular scanner-grade image.
  • Pixel-Perfect Corner Zoom: Enable Zoom Area to see an instant 3x magnifying loupe under your fingers or mouse cursor for exact corner placement.
  • PDF & Image Support: Upload standard photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) or multi-page PDF documents.
  • Direct Print & Instant Download: Preview your straightened document on the print sheet and send it straight to your printer or save it as a high-resolution file.
  • 100% Private & In-Browser: All processing is done locally on your device via HTML5 Canvas. Your sensitive IDs and documents are never uploaded to any remote server.
