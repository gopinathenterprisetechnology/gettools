Free Online Passport Size Photo Maker for Cyber Cafes & Students

GET Tools Passport Photo Maker is a specialized client-side utility built for cyber cafe operators, CSC VLEs, and students applying for competitive exams, job vacancies, and official IDs. It eliminates the need for expensive software like Adobe Photoshop by generating print-ready passport photo sheets directly inside your browser.

Why Use This Passport Photo Tool?

  • Standard Indian & Global Dimensions: Formatted to exact 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (approx 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI).
  • Date of Photo (DOP) & Name Stamp: Automatically print candidate name and photograph date at the bottom of each photo, complying with SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and State PSC guidelines.
  • Multiple Print Formats: Print 6 or 8 copies on standard 4×6 inch glossy photo paper, or 12 to 24 copies on A4 photo sheets with clear cutting guides.
  • 100% Privacy Protection: Photos are rendered in browser canvas memory. Zero photos are uploaded or stored.
