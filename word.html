Best Free Online Word & Character Counter Tool

GET Tools Word Counter is a free, fast, and real-time text analysis tool created to help writers, bloggers, students, digital marketers, and developers accurately count words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs.

Why Word Count Matters

In modern digital writing, maintaining word count limits is critical for search engine optimization (SEO), academic papers, social media posts, and online job applications. Our instant counter ensures you stay within exact length requirements.

Key Features:

  • Real-Time Dynamic Counting: Watch counts update automatically with zero latency as you type.
  • Reading Time Estimation: Based on the standard 200 words-per-minute reading pace.
  • Character Breakdown: Separate tracking for characters including and excluding spaces.
  • One-Click Actions: Fast copy to clipboard and instant clear buffer controls.
