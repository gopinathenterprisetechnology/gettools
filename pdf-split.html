Split & Extract PDF Pages Online

Extract specific pages or page ranges from any PDF document without sending private data to cloud servers.

Example: "1-3" extracts pages 1 to 3; "1, 4, 7" extracts pages 1, 4, and 7.

Free Client-Side PDF Splitter & Page Extractor

GET Tools PDF Splitter is an indispensable tool for students, advocates, accountants, and cyber cafe users who need to extract single pages or specific sections from large PDF files (like bank statements, court petitions, e-books, or government gazettes).

Key Features:

  • Flexible Page Syntax: Supports single pages (e.g. 2) and multi-page ranges (e.g. 2-7, 10).
  • Zero Server Uploads: PDF extraction runs 100% locally within your browser using WebAssembly and JavaScript.
  • Preserves Document Quality: Hyperlinks, vector graphics, fonts, and images are copied losslessly.
