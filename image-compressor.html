Free Online Image Compressor & Resizer with Exact KB Control

GET Tools Image Compressor Pro is a powerful and easy-to-use online image optimization tool designed to compress images to an exact KB size without losing quality. You can reduce image size to 50KB, 100KB, 200KB or any custom size required for exam forms, government portals, job applications, and website uploads.

Compress Images to Exact KB Size

Our advanced compression algorithm allows you to enter a target file size in KB and automatically adjusts image quality and dimensions to keep the final output within your selected limit. Unlike basic compressors, this tool ensures the compressed file does not exceed your specified size.

Resize Images with Pixel, mm, cm and DPI Control

You can resize images using Pixel, Millimeter (mm), or Centimeter (cm) units with custom DPI settings. This feature is especially useful for print-ready documents, ID photos, passport size photos, and professional printing requirements.

Convert JPG, PNG and WEBP Formats Easily

Instantly convert between JPG, PNG, and WEBP formats while compressing. You can maintain aspect ratio, adjust quality manually, and download optimized images individually or in bulk ZIP format.

Secure, Fast & Browser-Based Processing

All image processing happens directly in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security. The tool works smoothly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
