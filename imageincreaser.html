Advanced Image Size Increaser

Increase the file size (KB) of one or multiple images to your exact target without losing quality.

Upload Images & Enter Target KB

Select one or more images (JPG/PNG) and enter your desired minimum KB. The quality remains unchanged.

How It Works

  • Upload one or multiple images (JPEG, JPG, PNG) from your device.
  • Enter your required target size in KB (e.g., for online form submissions).
  • Click Increase Size to process the images safely to that exact file size.
  • The process is instant, keeps your resolution original, and runs 100% locally.
  • Click Download All Images to save the adjusted photos back to your device.
