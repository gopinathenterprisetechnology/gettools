Why Combine Photo and Signature for Online Job Forms?

Many government recruitment portals (including SSC, State Police, Railway RRB, and Public Service Commissions) require applicants to upload a single combined image containing both their passport photograph and handwritten signature.

Key Features:

  • Standard Proportions: Automatically scales the photograph to standard passport 3:4 ratio and aligns the signature proportionally underneath.
  • Candidate Name & DOP Stamping: Optionally print candidate name and photograph date in between or on top for form compliance.
  • Target Size Optimization: Enter your portal limit (e.g. 50 KB or 100 KB) and the tool automatically adjusts JPEG compression.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: All images are processed locally within browser memory. No data is sent to external servers.
