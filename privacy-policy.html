Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 February 2026

1. Introduction

At GET Tools (operated by Gopinath Enterprise & Technology), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Client-Side Data Processing

Our web utilities operate entirely within your browser. When you use tools such as our Image Compressor, PDF Compressor, Word Counter, or Password Generator, your files, images, and text inputs are never transmitted to our web servers or stored in any database.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identification information directly. However, third-party advertising and monetization partners (such as Adsterra and Cuelinks) may utilize standard cookies or web beacons to deliver relevant advertisements and monitor network traffic.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

Third-party vendors may place cookies on your browser to personalize ads based on visits to this website and other sites across the internet. You can manage or disable cookies at any time via your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement standard web security best practices. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure. You acknowledge this inherent limitation when browsing online.

6. User Consent

By using the GET Tools website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
