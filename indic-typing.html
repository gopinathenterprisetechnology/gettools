Free Online Indic Language Typing & Transliteration

GET Tools Indic Typing Tool is an effortless phonetic converter designed for cyber cafe operators, bloggers, translators, and students who need to compose applications, biodatas, official letters, or social media posts in Indian languages.

Supported Languages & Transliteration:

  • Devanagari Scripts: Hindi (हिन्दी), Marathi (मराठी), Sanskrit (संस्कृतम्).
  • Eastern Scripts: Bengali (বাংলা), Assamese (অসমীয়া), Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ).
  • Dravidian Scripts: Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Malayalam (മലയാളം).
  • Western & Northern Scripts: Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Punjabi Gurmukhi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ), Urdu (اردو).
